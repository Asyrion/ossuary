# M4.5 Bestiary: plan

The owner's ask (2026-09-30): "a whole host more" creatures built in code, small critters, fantasy creatures and
elementals, and new kinds of enemy behaviour (GDD: Combat and enemies, The Bestiary; ideas in `docs/ideas.md`,
Bestiary). Taken up after M4.2 shipped, on the owner's "After that continue" (2026-10-01).

## Decisions (2026-10-01; the owner can change any of it)

1. **Looks first, then foes.** As with the first ten: every candidate is drawn up in code as a look (the dev menu's
   Galleries tab), the owner chooses, and only the chosen become foes. The rules of the ten hold: round, readable
   shapes; big eyes on the critters; a school's light; smaller than the mage (elites aside); a floor each.
2. **The candidates are the idea bank's, less the ones M4.1 already made foes** (the Wickmouse, Chime Bat, Gargoyle
   Whelp, Moonstag Fawn, Rootling, Storm Ray and Void Mote): ten critters, eight fantasy creatures and thirteen elementals,
   thirty-one looks. Names are placeholders.
3. **Each candidate comes with the kind of fight it would bring** (a new behaviour from the idea bank's enemy types
   where it has one: splitter, swarm, turret, shielded, trapper, kiter, leech, mirror, tethered pair, summoner), so the
   owner chooses fights as well as faces.
4. **Elementals follow the idea bank's rules, to be tried when they are made foes:** their own school's status heals
   them, the opposite school hurts twice over (Ember and Frost, Hallow and Umbral), and they touch the room as their
   element does.

## Steps

1. **The critters' looks** (10, built): Inkling, Rime Snail, Spiderling, Firefly Cloud, Rubble Crab, Dust Puff, Frost Hare,
   Puffcap, Ember Wasp, Glass Newt.
2. **The fantasy creatures' looks** (8, built): Basilisk Hatchling, Grimoire Mimic, Brine Serpent, Phoenix Chick (elite),
   Griffin Fledgling (elite), Shadow Fox, Harpy Chick, Salt Golemling.
3. **The elementals' looks** (13, built): Cinder Sprite, Magma Blob, Hearth Golem (elite), Snowball Sprite, Glacier Golem
   (elite), Spark Sprite, Thunderhead, Lodestone Golem, Choir Wisp, Radiant Orb, Gloom Sprite, Puddle Elemental, Rubble
   Golem.
   All thirty-one are `src/actors/bestiaryLooks.ts` (`BESTIARY_OPTIONS`: id, kind, school, floor, look), built from the
   arcane parts like the first ten; the Galleries tab's "Bestiary candidates" stands them in rows by kind
   (`showBestiary` in `modelOptions.ts`, `__game.bestiaryOptions(ids?)`); `STR.bestiary` names each and says the fight it
   would bring (typed so every candidate must have one). A browser test stands them all inside a room.
4. **The owner chooses** in the Galleries tab (the bestiary candidates in rows by kind, each named with the fight it
   would bring).
5. **The chosen become foes**: data in `data/foes.json`, the new behaviours in `src/actors/foes.ts`, a floor each and
   the floors' rolls, sounds, browser tests; the elemental rules in the pure `foeData.ts`.
6. **Close**: the GDD as built, CLAUDE.md, the rules reviewer, the room inspector's sweep, and a build for the owner
   when asked.
