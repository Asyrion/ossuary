# M4.5 Bestiary: plan

The owner's ask (2026-09-30): "a whole host more" creatures built in code (small critters, fantasy creatures and
elementals) and new kinds of enemy behaviour (GDD: Combat and enemies, The Bestiary; ideas in `docs/ideas.md`,
Bestiary). Taken up after M4.2 shipped, on the owner's "After that continue" (2026-10-01).

## Decisions

**2026-10-01, first round.** As with the first ten: draw every candidate in code as a look (the dev menu's
Galleries tab), let the owner choose, and make foes only of the chosen. Thirty-one candidates from the idea bank (ten
critters, eight fantasy creatures, thirteen elementals), each with the kind of fight it would bring.

**2026-10-01, the owner's answer.** All thirty-one, and four more: **thirty-five foes**. The fights and floors are
left to us ("be creative"). The looks are redone: the first drafts were cute little things with big eyes, and many
didn't read as what they were meant to be. The owner's intent was **fantasy animals and creatures** (not mainly
skeletons, ghouls and the like), so the redesign follows these rules:

1. **Readable at a glance.** Each one is the animal or creature it is named after, drawn from its defining shapes: a
   spider's eight jointed legs, a crab's claws, a snail's spiral shell, a wasp's bands and sting, a griffin's eagle head on
   a lion's body. Its school shows in its light (eyes, runes, crystals, flame), not in a cartoon face.
2. **Fierce, not cute.** No big round eyes, no smiles. Eyes are small glowing slits under a brow. Heads have fangs,
   beaks, mandibles and horns, and bodies carry claws, spines and armour. The proportions are an animal's.
3. **Their real size.** No longer smaller than the mage by rule. Beasts stand between the mage's knee and chest, or
   are as long as the mage is tall. Golems and elites are as tall as the mage or taller.
4. **No undead.** Beasts, mythic creatures and elementals only.
5. **Not cute in name either.** The "-ling", "chick", "fledgling" and "sprite" names go. Names stay placeholders until
   M8.
6. **Screenshots before committing.** The owner sees the redesigned looks before they are committed.
7. **Fights.** Each foe brings a new kind of fight from the idea bank (splitter, swarm, turret, shielded, trapper, kiter,
   mirror, summoner, pack and more). Each fight has a magic take: a school or a mechanism that changes it.
8. **Elementals follow the idea bank's rules.** Their own school's status heals them, and the opposite school does
   double damage (Ember and Frost, Hallow and Umbral, Storm and water). Each touches the room the way its element does.

## Steps

1. **The looks redone, creature by creature** (35): the rules above. The Galleries tab's "Bestiary candidates"
   stands them in a great hall by kind (beasts, creatures, elementals), with the mage for scale. **Screenshots to the
   owner, committed only on approval.**
2. **The foe framework** (built with floor 1): `foeKit.ts` (what a behaviour is written against, shared by `foes.ts` and
   `bestiaryFoes.ts`; a blow's direction reaches `struck`, a glyph's blast reaches `blasted`), `bestiaryGround.ts` (webs and
   ink that slow the mage, fire burning webs through `world/groundHooks.ts`; lobs thrown in an arc), packs that share one
   mind (`pack` in the data, `spawnFoe`), a foe's own look (`look`), a lark's song (`sung`). Still to come as the floors
   need them: the elemental rules in the pure `foeData.ts`, gaits for the walkers, a wasp's nest and a phoenix's ashes.
3. **Floor 1's seven foes** (built): the dropper, splitter, tome, swarm, singer, mantis and crab in `bestiaryFoes.ts`, in
   floor 1's rolls beside M4's four, with sounds and a browser test of each one's mechanic.
4. **Floor 2's seven.**
5. **Floor 3's seven.**
6. **Floor 4's seven.**
7. **Floor 5's seven.**
8. **The floors' rolls and balance.** The `foes` weights in `data/floors.json`, an auto-play pass per floor, and the dev
   menu's lists (which come from the data).
9. **Close.** The GDD as built, CLAUDE.md, the rules reviewer, the room inspector's sweep, and a build for the owner
   when asked.
