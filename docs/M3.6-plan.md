# M3.6 plan: Rooms of Eda

From the first play-test (2026-09-28): rooms feel and look too much like Isaac. The owner chose three directions,
in this order: **A. architecture inside the cell, B. themed rooms, C. mechanisms that answer to magic**, with
new objects and piles alongside; doors may move off-centre. The rooms, objects and twists were left to us
("surprise me"), so the decisions below are proposals the owner can veto. The ideas behind them are in
`docs/ideas.md` (Rooms of Eda, Objects and piles). This file tracks the build; the design moves into the GDD
(Floors and rooms) step by step. Each step ends playable, with unit and browser tests passing, and is pushed.

M3.6 runs beside M3.5 (the asset pipeline), which waits on the owner's style tests and pilot assets. The
balance pass of M3 still waits on play-tests; M3.6 doesn't change magic's numbers.

**Done when:** a floor reads as a place in a dungeon, not a set of arenas: rooms have architecture (carved
outlines, dressed walls, doors off the middle), a third or more of the fights take place in themed rooms that
play differently, and the rooms' objects and mechanisms answer to the mage's magic.

## Decisions (2026-09-28)

1. **Carved outlines.** In a template, `x` may mark wall anywhere, not only outside an L: along the edge it
   carves the outline (corners cut away, a stepped apse, a bay, a narrow neck), inside a hall it stands as a
   block of wall (a partition, a thick pier). The shape's cells and door slots stay as they are, so the
   generator keeps working on cells; the validator keeps door lanes clear and every floor tile reachable. To
   carve without boxes overlapping (two faces in one plane flicker), the wall builder moves from wall runs to
   a grid of wall tiles merged into boxes: tall where the wall stands north of the floor or beside it, low
   where it stands south of it, as now.
2. **Dressed walls.** Walls stop being flat brick: pilasters stand out from the north walls at intervals,
   niches are set into them (a skull, a candle stub, an urn, a burial shelf of bones), and long walls get
   buttresses. What a floor's walls carry comes from its look in `data/floors.json` and, from step 3, the
   room's theme. Dressing is visual; it never blocks a door or a torch.
3. **Doors off the middle.** After the templates are chosen, each door moves along its cell edge to an offset
   where both rooms' templates leave the lanes clear: −4, −2, 0, +2 or +4 tiles on north and south edges, −2, 0 or
   +2 on east and west edges (narrow corridors keep what fits). The chance per door is per floor
   (`doorShift` in `data/floors.json`, about a half). Torches, the minimap and the mage's arrival follow the door.
4. **New objects** (template letters in brackets; tile behaviours as in ideas.md):

   | Object | Kind | Behaviour |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | Bone pile (`b`) | Low, breakable | A bolt scatters it; drops from the poorer `remains` pool |
   | Skull heap (`k`) | Low, breakable | Two hits; a skull rolls loose; `remains` pool |
   | Grave sacks (`s`) | Low, breakable | Spill one to three small things (`bundle` pool) |
   | Oil cask (`c`) | Tall, breakable | Breaks into a pool of oil on its tile and the free tiles round it |
   | Rubble (`r`) | Low | Only a glyph's blast clears it |
   | Grave roots (`t`) | Tall, flammable | Fire burns them away in a few seconds |
   | Thorn briar (`v`) | Soft, flammable | Hurts the mage (half a heart) and walkers passing through; burns |
   | Cobwebs (`:`) | Soft, flammable | Slow the mage and walkers; burn in a flash |
   | Glowcaps (`g`) | Low, reactive | Soft light; struck, they burst into spores; fire in the spores flashes (a small blast) |
   | Pews (`p`) | Low | Side by side they make one pew; bolts fly over them |

   "Low" things block walking; bolts (the mage's and foes') fly over them, unless the thing breaks to a bolt
   (piles), which stops the bolt as it breaks. So pews and rubble shape movement, not shooting. Floor scatter without collision (moss, loose pages, candle stubs, roots on the walls,
   rubble at the foot of a wall) is dressing, placed by the room's theme.
5. **Poorer loot for piles.** A `remains` pool in `data/loot.json`: mostly nothing, sometimes an obol or a glyph
   stone, rarely a mana vial or half a heart, never keys or artifacts; grave sacks spill from a `bundle` pool
   (one to three of the same small things). The economy simulation in `tests/unit/loot.test.ts` covers them.
6. **Themed rooms.** A template can carry a `theme` (`data/themes.json`: wall dressing, floor scatter, light
   tint, what the room's twist is). Eight themes, two or three templates each, spread over the floors' looks:

   | Theme | Floors | Its twist |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | Chapel nave | 1, 2 | Pews, an altar in a carved apse; lighting the altar's candles blesses the next casts |
   | Ossuary | 1, 3 | Skull niches glow in the school last cast; bone heaps rise as rotlings if the fight drags |
   | Scriptorium | 1 | Desks and heaps of scrolls that burn fast; now and then a torn page in a heap |
   | Pilgrims' rest | 1, 2 | Bedrolls, sacks and a cold fire; a quiet room with loot in the piles, sometimes a sleeper |
   | Flooded cistern | 2 | Deep water between stepping stones and pillars; Frost makes paths, Shock runs through it all |
   | Fungal grotto | 2 | Glowcaps, moss and dripstones; spores that catch fire |
   | Collapsed crypt | 2, 3 | Rubble, roots through the ceiling, a shaft of daylight; a blast brings more of the ceiling down |
   | Embalmers' hall | 3 | Slabs, oil casks and hooks; one spark and the room burns |

   About two fights in five take place in themed rooms (weights per floor); the rest keep the generic templates,
   which get the architecture of step 1. Themes never change a room's type: a chapel nave is a fight.
7. **Mechanisms** (letters in brackets), each answering to magic through the hooks rooms already use (`fireAt`,
   `frostAt`, `shockAt`, a bolt striking, a glyph's blast):

   | Mechanism | What it does |
   | --- | --- |
   | Brazier (`f`) | Fire lights it; a bolt of the mage's flying through the flame catches fire (Burn) |
   | Candle stand (`l`) | Fire lights it; every stand in a room lit opens the room's reliquary, or drives off the dark |
   | Bell (`a`) | A bolt rings it: every foe in the room reels for a moment (a cooldown) |
   | Rune stone (`n`) | A spell of its school charges it; charged, it pulses that status round it for the fight |
   | Lodestone pillar (`y`) | Shock leaps between lodestones in sight of each other, hurting what stands between |
   | Hanging censer (`*`) | A bolt cuts its chain; it falls, hurts what is under it and spills burning oil |

   Four more themes use them: the bell chamber (a bell), the frozen mausoleum (rime floor, rune stones of Frost),
   the reliquary gallery (candle stands and a sealed reliquary) and the cloister garth (briars, roots and a dead
   tree, braziers). Twelve themes in all.
8. **The dev menu** gets a row of objects and mechanisms to put down ahead of the mage, the themes' templates in
   its test rooms (from the data), and a switch to show door offsets on every door. `?room=<id>` shows any
   template, `?theme=chapel` makes every fight on a floor draw from one theme.
9. **Sound:** placeholder sounds for the new things (bones scattering, a cask splitting, roots and webs burning,
   spores flashing, a bell's toll, a brazier catching, a censer's chain snapping), played where they happen.

**Magic takes:** Isaac's rocks and poop become the dungeon's remains and furnishings, and almost all of them
answer to the mage's schools: fire clears roots, webs and briars and lights braziers and candles; frost makes
paths; shock runs through water and lodestones; glyphs bring down rubble and ceilings. Isaac's room layouts
become places with a use (a chapel, an ossuary, a cistern), each with one twist to learn.

## Steps

1. **Architecture (A).** *Built 2026-09-28:* walls from wall tiles (`walls.ts`), carving with `x`, dressed walls
   (`dressing.ts`), doors off the middle, thirteen carved templates (seven one-cell rooms, and long, tall, great and
   L halls); buttresses and burial shelves wait for the themes of step 3. The plan: wall-tile builder; `x` carves outlines and stands partitions; pilasters, niches,
   buttresses and burial shelves by floor look; door offsets in the generator (pure, unit-tested on hundreds of
   seeds: lanes clear on both sides, both rooms agree, every tile still reachable) and in the room builder, the
   minimap and arrival; at least twelve templates carved (cut corners, an apse, bays, partitions in halls).
   Dev menu: the door offset switch. Tests: validator, generator, e2e walk through every door of seeded floors.
2. **Objects and piles.** *Built 2026-09-29:* the ten objects, their loot, fire, spores and the cask's oil, sounds,
   the dev menu row, eight templates built round them (a charnel, a pilgrims' camp, a root crypt, a cask cellar, rows
   of pews, a glowcap grotto, a rubble hall and an overgrown great hall). Lore cards for piles, like urns, have none.
   The plan: The ten objects of decision 4 (tile kinds, collision classes, looks, strikes, burning,
   the oil cask's pool, glowcap spores), the `remains` and `bundle` loot pools and their simulation, floor
   scatter, placeholder sounds, lore for the new drops' first finding. Existing templates get some of them.
   Dev menu: the objects row.
3. **Themed rooms (B).** *Built 2026-09-29:* `data/themes.json`, the eight themes with 19 templates
   (`data/templates/themed.json`, one-cell rooms and halls), their looks and scatter, stalagmites and embalmers'
   slabs, desks and scroll heaps, the twists that need no mechanism (rising bones, pages, sleepers, the cave-in), a
   toast with the room's name, the dev menu's themes row and `?theme=`. The chapel's twist waits for candle stands.
   The plan: `data/themes.json` and the `theme` on templates; the first eight themes with two or
   three templates each (twenty or more new templates), their wall dressing, scatter and light; their twists
   that need no mechanism; the themes' weights per floor. Dev menu: themes in the test rooms, `?theme=`.
4. **Mechanisms (C).** *Built 2026-09-29:* the six mechanisms of decision 7 plus the reliquary and the dead tree
   (`src/world/mechanisms.ts`, tuning in `CFG.mech`), heard through `elementAt`, `strikeMechanism` and `boltThrough`;
   the four new themes with two templates each (bell chamber, frozen mausoleum, reliquary gallery, cloister garth)
   and three plain rooms round a mechanism (a brazier hall, a censer crypt, a rune circle); the chapel's blessing
   (every candle stand lit: six casts harder, in gold), candle stands in a dark room lifting the dark, the
   mausoleum's rime (its dead wait in ice, its water is ice, its rune stones hold frost, the mage slides) and the ossuary's watching skulls.
   Dev menu: the mechanisms in the objects row, and Fire, Frost and Shock buttons that land ahead of the mage.
   *Checks (CLAUDE.md, Agents and skills):* the room inspector on the new mechanisms and the
   four new themes when they are built, the rules reviewer before the PR, then the ship skill when the owner asks
   to merge. The plan: The six mechanisms of decision 7 and the four themes that use them; the twists that need
   them (the chapel's candles, the ossuary's glowing niches). Dev menu: the mechanisms in the objects row.
5. **Close.** *Checks:* the room inspector over every theme, `carved`, `props` and a floor of each look, before
   the play-test build. The plan: GDD (Floors and rooms, Pickups and room types, the roadmap), CLAUDE.md, the idea bank, a look at
   every theme in the browser, performance on a great hall full of objects, and a build for the play-tests. With
   the build, the owner asked (2026-09-29) for a play-test list on the session's Plan panel: what was added in M3.6
   and what to look out for and try while playing. Polish left from step 4's inspections: the garth's daylight shaft
   in an L's inner corner (and its open top), torch glints on cistern water that read as a wisp, the candle stand as
   tall as the mage though bolts fly over it, ice that keeps the water's ripples, the bell chamber plain apart from
   its bell, a censer's pot over the mage's face a tile north of it, the ossuary skulls' tiny eyes; the inspect
   tool's floor banner and lore cards in its shots. For the owner: a reliquary whose candle stands were all knocked
   over can never open.
