# M4.10 Weaves: plan

Proposed on 2026-10-01 at the owner's ask ("add more combinations like these", after Steam was built; GDD: Depth for
what's built, M4.10). It waits on the owner's yes, and its place in the order is theirs to move. It comes after M4.8,
whose reactions, Black Ice and codex it builds on.

M4.8 makes any two schools react on a foe or a tile. This milestone adds two more kinds of combination: **weaves**,
where one spell strikes what another spell left in the room, and **chains**, where what a reaction leaves behind meets
a third school. It also designs Verdant's two pairs with Hallow and Umbral for M5. Every weave and chain has its name
rise when it happens and is marked in the spell book once found (GDD pillar: Nothing silent).

## Decisions to take with the owner

1. **The ten weaves and seven chains, and their names** (placeholders until M8). Each one was picked because it works
   through something already built: `room.barriers` (the ice wall), the seal and its burning ground, Consecrate's zone,
   Wardlight's motes, the Static Mantle, a toll's mark, steam clouds, Black Ice, the Overload's blast, the Shatter's
   shards and the Eclipse's black sun.
2. **Whether weaves cost anything.** In this plan they are free: the cost is having both spells slotted and setting them
   up in the right order. The other choice is that a weave spends the lasting spell at once (the wall or seal is gone).
3. **Verdant's Bloom and Blight.** They are designed here and built with M5's Verdant school.

## Steps

1. **The rule table.**
   - `src/magic/weaves.ts` (pure, unit-tested): a table from (what was left, what strikes it) to a weave or chain, with
     its tuning in `data/magic.json` (`weaves`, `chains`).
   - One hook, `weaveAt(left, by, x, z)`, called from the places in the tables above. It sets the weave off and raises
     its name (`STR.reactions`, in the school colours of both).
   - The dev menu: Magic gets "Weaves here", one button per weave or chain, which lays the first spell or reaction ahead
     of the mage and strikes it with the second.
2. **The wall and seal weaves** (Steam Curtain, Conductor, Overload Seal, Black Pyre).
   - Steam Curtain uses `steamAt` along the wall's length (`magic/steam.ts`). Conductor and Overload Seal use the
     existing shock and blast code; Black Pyre uses the burn's `black` flag.
   - Looks in the house style of `docs/effects.md`, in `spellFx.ts`. Browser tests: each one, set up and struck.
3. **The holy ground, mote, mantle and toll weaves** (Eclipse Ground, Hallowed Hail, Lantern Fan, Live Wade,
   Refraction, Toll Chain). Same pattern: one browser test each.
4. **The chains.**
   - The steam clouds take a school (`charge(cloud, school)` in `steam.ts`): Thundercloud, Incense, Miasma.
   - Black Melt is a pool of a new kind in `pools.ts` (`black`), burning like oil but with Blackfire.
   - Hailburst, Molten Glass and Corona are added to the Overload's blast, the Shatter's shards and the Eclipse's fall
     in `statusFx.ts`.
   - Unit tests of the rules; browser tests of each chain.
5. **The room joins in.**
   - A brazier lit by Blackfire burns violet, and bolts through it carry a hex (`boltThrough`).
   - A rune stone struck by two schools in turn sets off their reaction at its foot.
   - A bell rung by a shock rings a ring of sparks (`laySpark`).
   - These go through `elementAt` and `strikeMechanism` (`mechanisms.ts`).
6. **The codex.** Weaves and chains join M4.8's book: the wheel's spokes gain a ring of weaves, sealed until found, kept
   between runs (`memory.ts`). M3.7's panels list a spell's weaves under its reactions.
7. **Close.** Sounds in the schools' palettes (a weave sounds both). The GDD as built, CLAUDE.md, the rules reviewer,
   and the room inspector on the new looks (steam curtain, black pyre, molten glass, black melt).

**Done when** every weave and chain in the tables happens when set up, its name rises, it is marked in the book once
found, and the dev menu sets each one off.

## Verdant's last two pairs (for M5)

- **Bloom** (Verdant + Hallow): an overgrown foe sanctified flowers; when it falls its lily mends half a heart, once a
  room.
- **Blight** (Verdant + Umbral): the vines rot to thorns that hex the foe and creep to the nearest other foe.

With Wildfire, Frostbloom and Pollen Spark (GDD: Magic system, Verdant), all fifteen pairs of the six schools react.
