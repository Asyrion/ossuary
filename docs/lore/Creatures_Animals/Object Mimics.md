	- Chest-Mimic
		- A Chest-Mimic is a creature that disguises itself as a treasure chest, luring unsuspecting adventurers to open it and then attacking them with its sharp teeth and tongue. It has a wooden exterior that can blend in with different environments, such as dungeons, forests or caves. It can also mimic the sound of coins or jewels rattling inside to entice its prey. A Chest-Mimic is usually very greedy and will try to devour anything that comes near it, even if it is too big to fit in its mouth.
