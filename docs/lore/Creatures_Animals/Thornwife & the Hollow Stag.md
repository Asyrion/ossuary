**Name:** _Thornwife & the Hollow Stag_

---

### 🌿 _The Thornwife_

A parasitic-symbiont vine-creature, the Thornwife appears as a mass of blackened, thorn-studded tendrils wrapped in pale flowers that bloom only under moonlight. It originates from the deep barrow-forests of Oln Marrow, where the soil is rich with ancient rot and quiet madness.

- **Biology:** The Thornwife begins life as a creeping rootlet, nearly invisible beneath the underbrush. It seeks a host with a fractured soul—creatures on the brink of death or madness are preferred. Upon finding such a host, it entwines into their nervous system, feeding off spiritual dissonance rather than blood or flesh.
    
- **Behavior:** Alone, the Thornwife is slow-moving and blind, dependent on hosts to survive. However, it gifts its host unnatural senses—an ability to feel the heartbeat of nearby prey or perceive lies spoken aloud.
    
- **Reproduction:** When its host dies, the Thornwife releases its seed through a burst of spores from its withered blossoms. These spores root into nearby corpses, beginning the cycle anew.
    

---

### 🦌 _The Hollow Stag_

An antlered beast of faded nobility, the Hollow Stag is a once-proud species of forest guardian, now near-extinct. It wanders the Deadgrove—an expanse where the trees grow in warped spirals and weep black resin.

- **Physiology:** Towering at nine feet from hoof to antler tip, its skin is taut and pale, marked with ritual scars that glow faintly in fog. Its ribcage is visible beneath the hide, with hollowed-out cavities filled now by the Thornwife's thorns.
    
- **Mind:** The Thornwife sustains the Hollow Stag’s broken body and grants it a predatory cunning, but in return, the stag’s memories are slowly leached away. In its final years, it becomes a wandering revenant—silent, watchful, ghostlike.
    
- **Cultural Significance:** Some forest clans believe encountering the Hollow Stag signals an impending death. Others whisper that if one drinks from the dew on its antlers, they’ll dream of what the stag remembers—forgotten hunts, or blood-soaked rituals long buried.
    

---

### Symbiosis:

- The _Thornwife_ offers vitality, sharpened senses, and a new lease on life in exchange for flesh and memory.
    
- The _Hollow Stag_ gains prolonged life and strength but loses its soul by inches, becoming a vessel more than a beast.
