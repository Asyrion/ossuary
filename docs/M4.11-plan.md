# M4.11 Spell marks: plan

**Status: built 2026-10-01** (GDD: Spells, Spell marks). The owner's review of the looks is next.

The owner's ask (2026-10-01, after M4.6): "visual effects of the carried spells or learned spells: different auras or other
small visual details governed by the carried spells, the overall spell knowledge, or the knowledge of a school." M4.6
made the build visible through artifacts; this makes the magic visible on the mage too. Its place in the order is the
owner's to move.

## Decisions taken as this plan proposes them (the owner may change any)

1. **Looks that also tell.** Each mark shows something the player needs, so it is worth the screen. The spells in hand show
   whether each spell is ready, cooling down or short of mana, the same as the HUD's slots but at the mage, where the eye
   is.
2. **Small and in the school's light.** The marks stay near the hands and the feet, smaller than the transformations'
   looks, so the build's costume stays the main picture. Their light is the school's (`SCHOOL_LIGHT`) and their motion the
   school's own: embers rise, rime falls, sparks crackle, motes drift up, shadow sinks.
3. **Purely visual.** No gift comes with a mark. Attunement or mastery could give something in M6 (RPG growth). Each
   still says what it is the first time (GDD pillar: Nothing silent).
4. **Knowledge across runs shows from the first room**, since what the mage knows is kept between runs (M3.7).

## The marks

| Mark | Comes from | What shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spell in hand | Each slot's spell: Q at the casting hand, E at the off hand | A small light in the school's colour with the school's motion. Ready: full. Cooling down: faint, filling back, and a flash when ready again. Too little mana: it flickers and greys. |
| Master's ring | A slotted spell whose whole school the mage knows (every spell of it cast enough to be known well, the "used" stage) | A thin rune ring round that wrist in the school's light, turning |
| Attunement | Both slots holding spells of one school | The school's light round the whole mage: motes of its kind about the body and a soft glow. The first time in a run, a line: "Attuned to Ember". |
| Knowledge circle | Every spell known well, kept between runs | Standing still for a moment, a faint rune circle shows round the feet: five arcs, one per school, a rune for each spell known well, lit in its school's light; a school known in full closes its arc in a bright line. It fades as the mage moves. |

## Steps

1. **The pure rules** (`src/magic/marksData.ts`, unit-tested): the state of a spell in hand (`handState`: ready, cooling with
   how full, short); attunement (`attunedTo`); the knowledge circle (`circleOf`: per school, which spells are known well,
   and whether it is mastered); `masteredSchools`.
2. **The look** (`src/actors/spellMarks.ts`): the hand lights in world space at the hands each step (the bones the costumes
   use), the master's rings, the attunement aura, the knowledge circle; built once per school and reused; a line the first
   time a run is attuned and the first time a school is mastered.
3. **Dev menu and hooks**: a Spell marks section in the Magic tab (what shows; the circle shown now; attune by learning both
   spells of a school with the existing spell buttons; the knowledge row knows every spell or forgets all), and
   `__game.spellMarks`.
4. **Close**: tests (unit and a browser test), screenshots for the owner, the frame cost, the GDD as built, CLAUDE.md.

## As built

- The pure rules: `src/magic/marksData.ts` (`handState`, `attunedTo`, `circleOf`, `masteredSchools`, `knownWell`), unit-tested.
- The look: `src/actors/spellMarks.ts` (`updateSpellMarks` from heroVisual, at the hands through `handAt` in costume.ts;
  `knownWellNow` from castSpell says when a school is known in full; `marksNow` for the tests and the dev menu); tuning
  `CFG.marks`; lines in `STR.marks`; `G.run.attuned`.
- Dev menu (Magic tab): Spell marks (what shows; attune to each school, `attuneTo` in spells.ts; show the circle).
  `__game.spellMarks`, `__game.attune(school)`.
- Frame cost: 73 draw calls bare, 77 with two spells in hand, 79 attuned, 85 with the rings and the circle.

**Done when** the slotted spells show at the hands with their state, attunement and mastery show and say so, the
knowledge circle grows with what is known between runs, and the frame cost stays small (a few draw calls).
