# M4.6 Costumes and transformations: plan

Decided with the owner on 2026-09-30, after the Pilgrim's Cowl proved the idea (GDD: Artifacts, Costumes and
transformations; ideas.md: Costumes). Its place in the order is the owner's to move. M4.7 to M4.10 were built first; the
owner said "continue" after them (2026-10-01), and this is the next milestone on the roadmap.

Picking up an artifact shows on the mage, as Isaac's items do, in a high fantasy and magic way. Three artifacts of a kind
transform the mage for the rest of the run, with a look and a gift. And the mage, Asyrion, grows from the apprentice
towards the chaos mage as their power grows.

## Decisions taken as this plan proposes them (the owner may change any)

1. **Slots.** The GDD's seven (head, face, shoulders, back, hands, belt, aura), plus `feet` (the Pilgrim's Boots can't go
   anywhere else), plus the focus held in the casting hand, which is not a slot: the mage always holds exactly one.
2. **Pieces reuse the artifacts' own models where they can.** A focus, a lantern, a purse or a jar on the mage is the same
   model as on its pedestal (`artifactLook`), scaled and hung from a bone, so the HUD icon, the pedestal and the mage agree.
   Pieces that wrap the body (crowns, masks, mantles, manacles, boots) are built for the body, as the hood is.
3. **Which artifacts wear what**, in the table below: 52 of the 57. The companions (the Candle Moth, the Weeping Skull,
   Fen-Nex) and the two that already show round the mage (the Twin Vigil Candles' flames, the Salt Circle) wear nothing more.
4. **The eight transformations of the GDD's proposed table** (the Alchemist waits for M5's potions), each counting the
   artifacts tagged with its `family` in data/artifacts.json, as listed below.
5. **Shadowstep's blink** is a new action: Shift on the keyboard, the right trigger on a gamepad, with a cooldown.
6. **Asyrion's arc** in four stages at 3, 6, 9 and 12 artifacts held (`CFG.arc`): the chaos mage's pieces fill the
   slots no artifact holds, so the mage grows into the fire mage while the build still shows.

A transformation is for the rest of the run, even if an artifact of it is given up (a focus swapped back). Its look takes
several slots, the aura and a tint of the robes; the latest one formed wears its look, and every gift formed stays. A
banner and a lore card name it (GDD pillar: Nothing silent).

## Asyrion's arc

The run begins in the apprentice's look (the Tripo model as it is). As the mage holds more artifacts, the chaos mage's
pieces arrive, each in a slot no artifact holds: 3 held, bronze-worked bracers on both forearms; 6, a dark coat's collar and
shoulders worked in bronze; 9, red rune orbs in thin gold rings circling the mage; 12, a flame in the off hand and white
arcs of wind. Each stage reached says so in a line (Nothing silent). Levels (M6) will count towards it too.

## Steps

1. **The framework.** Slots and anchors (head, face, chest, both hands and forearms, hips, feet, and the mage's own
   group for the aura), measured once per model in its rest pose as now; the pure rules in `costumeData.ts` (which piece
   shows in each slot, cursed and cleansed looks, unit-tested); pieces built once and merged (a few draw calls each),
   their geometry and materials disposed when taken off. The focus in the casting hand. The dev menu: a Costumes section
   in the Mage tab (wear any piece, take all off), `__game.wear(id)`, `__game.costume`.
2. **The pieces worn from the artifacts' own models**: the foci, the belt, the back and most of the shoulders.
3. **The pieces built for the body**: the crowns, halo and antlers, the masks and lens, the mantles, sash and stole, the
   manacles and their chains, the bands and beads, the boots; the cursed and cleansed looks.
4. **Transformations**: the `family` tags, the pure rules (which have formed, unit-tested), the banner, lore card and
   sound, the looks (slots, aura, tint), and the eight gifts with their indicators. The dev menu forms each one.
5. **Asyrion's arc**: the four stages, their pieces and lines; the dev menu steps through them.
6. **Close**: screenshots of every piece and transformation on the mage for the owner, the browser tests, the frame cost
   (draw calls with every slot filled), the GDD as built, CLAUDE.md and the rules reviewer.

**Done when** most artifacts show on the mage, cursed pieces change when cleansed, each transformation forms at three of
its family with its look, banner and gift, the arc grows with the mage's power, and the frame cost stays in budget.
