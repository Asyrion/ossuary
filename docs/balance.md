# Balance

Written by `npm run balance` (tools/balance.ts, M4.9). Every foe fought alone in a great hall on its own floor by a bot that walks, shoots and casts its two spells whenever they are ready; 2 seed(s) each, fights cut off at 40 s (DNF). Each cell: the mean time to put it down · the mean hearts it cost. Flagged: a foe taking more than twice its floor's median, a school whose median on a floor is more than twice the floor's. Last run: 2026-10-01, 2 min.

**Reading it.** The bot casts in four directions as the mage does and walks in when out of reach, but it chalks no glyphs and stands in no steam: a foe hiding in water (the Cistern Lurker, the Brine Serpent) shows as DNF unless its spells bring it up, though a player has a blast, steam, a shock or a sluice to do it. A foe circling the mage (the Phoenix) is hard for it to line up on. Elites take longer by design. Each fight is one foe alone, so the floors' pressure dials (more foes, more elites) don't show here.
