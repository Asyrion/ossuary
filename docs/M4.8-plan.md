# M4.8 The full wheel: plan

Proposed on 2026-10-01 in the review the owner asked for after M4.5 (GDD: Depth for what's built, M4.8). It waits on the
owner's yes, and its place in the order is theirs to move.

Five schools make ten pairs, and four of them react today: Shatter, Overload, Eclipse and Blackfire (`Reaction` in
`src/magic/status.ts`, tuning in `data/magic.json`). Steam has been in the GDD's table since M3 but was never built. This
milestone builds Steam and five more reactions, so that any two schools meeting on a foe or a tile do something. The
mage learns them as it learns spells. M5's Verdant brings three more reactions (with Ember, Frost and Storm). Its pairs
with Hallow and Umbral are still to design, with it.

## Decisions to take with the owner

1. **The five new reactions and their names** (placeholders until M8): Searing (Ember + Hallow), Prism (Frost + Hallow),
   Verdict (Storm + Hallow), Black Ice (Frost + Umbral) and Void Spark (Storm + Umbral). There are other ways to fill a
   pair. Each one here was picked because it works on systems already built: guards, the cantrip, the shock's leap,
   rime's slide and the hex's mana.
2. **Steam blinds both sides.** Foes inside the cloud can't aim, and foes that need to see lose the mage inside it. In this
   plan it helps the mage in both ways.

## Steps

1. **Steam** (Ember + Frost).
   - Rules: `steam` in `status.ts`'s reaction table; tuning in `data/magic.json` (radius, seconds).
   - Look: `steamFx` in `spellFx.ts`, pooled, in the house style of `docs/effects.md`.
   - On foes. A foe inside the cloud shoots wide (`spawnShot` takes a spread while its shooter stands in steam).
     `clearSight` and the statue's and eye's sight checks fail across the cloud.
   - On the room. `fireAt` on frozen water and `frostAt` on burning oil make steam too (`pools.ts`).
   - Tests: unit tests of the rule, and a browser test that a Radiant Eye loses the mage in steam.
2. **Searing** (Ember + Hallow).
   - For a few seconds the foe's `guard` is forced to 1. This opens the crab's and snail's shells, the eye's lids, the
     mimic's clay and the Hearth Golem's fire. On a War-Tiger it knocks off one plate (`breakPlate`).
   - One rule in `damageEnemy` reads `e.seared`, so the behaviours that set `guard` need no change.
   - Look: a golden crack over the foe.
   - Test: a shut Radiant Eye, seared, takes a full blow.
3. **Prism** (Frost + Hallow).
   - A frozen, sanctified foe becomes a prism: a cantrip bolt that strikes it splits into three (`fireSpellBolt`'s
     split, as the Splitting artifact does), once a bolt.
   - Look: the ice turns clear with a rainbow edge.
   - Test: one bolt into a prism comes out as three.
4. **Verdict** (Storm + Hallow).
   - When a sanctified foe is shocked, the shock leaps at once to every sanctified foe in the room (`shockLeap` with no
     range limit for those foes).
   - Look: `strikeFx` lightning from the sky to each of them.
   - Test: three sanctified foes, one shocked, and all three are struck.
5. **Black Ice** (Frost + Umbral).
   - A patch of black ice forms under the foe (`layGround(room, 'blackice', ...)` in `bestiaryGround.ts`).
   - Foes on it slide and can't stop, using M4.7's ground-on-foes rule. If M4.8 comes first, the slide is written here.
     The mage crosses it at full speed.
   - Fire melts it.
   - Test: a walker on black ice keeps its momentum into a hazard.
6. **Void Spark** (Storm + Umbral).
   - Each leap of the shock carries one hex stack to the foe it reaches (`shockLeap`).
   - A foe that falls while shocked gives the mage mana (`CFG.mana`).
   - Test: a shocked, hexed foe struck passes a hex to its neighbour.
7. **Reactions on tiles.**
   - The pairs that can live on the floor react there too. A shock through burning oil gives an Overload at the pool's
     middle. A hex on Consecrate's holy ground eclipses what stands in it. Fire on frozen water makes Steam (step 1).
   - This is a table in `pools.ts` and `shapes.ts`, reached through the hooks every element already calls (`fireAt`,
     `frostAt`, `shockAt`, `umbralAt`).
8. **The codex.**
   - `memory.ts` keeps the reactions found (`ossuary.knowledge`, beside the spells), and the first time a reaction
     happens its name rises in gold.
   - The spells tab's book gains a wheel of the five schools (Verdant's spoke sealed until M5). Its spokes light as
     reactions are found, and choosing one describes it. M3.7's panels mark a spell's reactions as found or not.
   - The dev menu: Magic gains "Reactions here", one button per reaction, which sets it off on a dummy ahead of the
     mage. The knowledge row also finds or forgets the reactions.
9. **Close.** Sounds in each school's palette. The GDD's reaction table as built, CLAUDE.md, the rules reviewer, and
   the room inspector on steam and black ice.

**Done when** any two of the five schools on one foe set off a reaction whose name rises, each reaction does what its
row says, the tile reactions work, and the book's wheel fills in as reactions are found, kept between runs.
