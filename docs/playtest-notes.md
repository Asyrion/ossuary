# Play-test notes

What the owner finds in play, newest first. Each round steers the reset's next phases (`docs/RESET-plan.md`).

## 2026-10-12: the owner's view before the reset (R0)

The owner did not play a separate run for R0: what they had said opening the reset stands as the round.

**What delights:**
- The game itself: the owner likes what came out of the many sessions and wants it kept, made cleaner, not rebuilt.
- Magic as the heart of it, and the RPG growth around it.
- Moonlighter's village between runs, where the dungeon's finds are sold: a spur for the Vigil, given a magic twist
  (`docs/ideas.md`, Between runs, after Moonlighter).

**What bothers:**
- The graphics: the owner is fairly unhappy with them, which they put down to the way assets were made so far. The
  answer is the art line in R3 (pre-rendered pixel-art sprites from Tripo 3D, Godot-Tamer's pipeline).
- The project lost its path across many sibling sessions: it needs to be made clean, modern and good to look at, step by
  step.
- The story is thin: the goal is a story-driven roguelite with RPG elements and a focus on magic, original and not a copy
  of Hades (R4).
