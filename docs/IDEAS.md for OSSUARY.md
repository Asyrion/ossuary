# Current Playtests List for Step 4:
For this play-test, the step 4 things most worth watching:
- [] **Braziers:** shoot a cantrip through a lit brazier. The bolt should catch fire and burn what it hits.
- [] **Candle stands:** light every stand in a chapel nave or reliquary gallery, with any fire spell or a glyph. Check that the reliquary opens, the next six casts are blessed, and a dark room stays lit.
- [] **Bells:** a bolt rings the bell and every foe should reel. The bell then needs about 7 seconds before it rings again.
- [] **Frozen mausoleum (floor 2):** its foes wait in ice until you hit or burn them, and the mage slides on the floor. Does the slide feel good or annoying?
- [] **Lodestones and censers:** a shock spell near a lodestone arcs to the other lodestones and hurts you too if you stand between them. A bolt through a censer's chain drops it in burning oil.
