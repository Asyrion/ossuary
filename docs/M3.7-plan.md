# M3.7 Spell knowledge: plan

The owner's idea (2026-09-29), decided in the GDD (Spells: Spell knowledge): a library page is taken the moment the
mage touches it, so the choice of three is blind. A small panel over a page, filled in as the mage comes to know
the spell, makes it a choice; the inventory's spells tab grows into a book by school. What the mage knows is kept
between runs, in the browser until the hub between runs takes it over.

## Decisions (2026-09-29)

1. **Stages.** A spell is *seen* when its page is first approached, *learned* when a page of it is taken, *used*
   after about ten casts over all runs (`CFG.knowledge.usedCasts`), and *upgraded* when runes or school mastery
   change it (M6: the stage exists, nothing reaches it yet).
2. **What each stage shows.** Seen: name, school in its colour, the one-line effect (`text`). Learned: adds mana,
   cooldown, damage and statuses. Used: adds the lore and the reactions it can set off (Frost and Storm: Shatter;
   Ember and Storm: Overload). Upgraded: adds what changed it (M6).
3. **Kept between runs.** One record per spell (seen, learned, casts, upgrades) in `localStorage` under
   `ossuary.knowledge`, read at boot and written when it changes; a private window or blocked storage just starts
   from nothing. It moves into the hub's grimoire later.
4. **The page panel.** Within about two tiles of a grimoire page (library, boss, loose) a small HTML panel stands over
   the nearest page, following it on screen, at the spell's stage. Walking into the page still takes it. A shelf
   page's price stays on its tag.
5. **The spells tab.** The two carried slots stay on top. Below, one section per school, shown once a spell of that
   school is learned: every spell of it the mage has met (carried ones marked Q or E, given-up ones dimmed, seen-only
   ones faint), and a sealed entry for each one not met yet. Each description is filled to its stage.
6. **One renderer.** The page panel and the inventory share one description of a spell at a stage.
7. **Dev menu.** Know every spell (all used), forget everything; the knowledge row shows how many spells are at each
   stage.

**Magic take:** knowledge is the mage's growth that carries over: learning a spell's reactions is learning to
combine the schools, which is the game's heart.

## Steps (built 2026-09-29)

1. **The knowledge store.** Pure rules in `src/magic/knowledge.ts` (stages, what a record becomes on seeing,
   learning, casting; reactions from a spell's statuses) with unit tests; the browser side (load, save, the events
   from pages and casting). Dev menu buttons and hooks (`__game.knowledge`, `__game.know(...)`).
2. **The page panel.** The shared spell description at a stage; the panel over the nearest page, placed each frame;
   seeing a page on approach. Browser tests: the panel's content at each stage.
3. **The spells tab by school.** Sections, faint and sealed entries, descriptions by stage. Browser test.
4. **Close.** GDD and CLAUDE.md, the rules reviewer, and a build for the owner (when the owner asks to ship).
