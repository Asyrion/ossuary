# M4.2 Beneath the Gate: plan

The owner's ask (2026-09-30), after reading what their Eda lore drop (PR 24: notes and 27 images) suggests for the game
(`docs/ideas.md`, The Eda lore drop): "I like all your strongest ideas takeaways. Let us do all of them and add them to the
GDD if they fit." They are in the GDD (Vision: Setting; Combat and enemies: Bosses from the Eda lore; Magic system: a
sixth school; Artifacts: Asyrion's arc, Fen-Nex; Potions and alchemy: Dr. Jelly's laboratory; Floors and rooms: From the
Eda lore; Pickups and room types: the Wandering Merchant; RPG progression: the Vigil). This milestone builds the ones that
belong now; the rest go into the milestones they fit (below).

## Decisions (2026-09-30; the owner can change any of it)

1. **The setting comes into the game's text.** The dungeon is the Uzulim Wardens' vault beneath the Gate of Uzulim, in the
   Pass of Buluiain; the chapel above is theirs; the Hollow Warden is a fallen Warden; the mage is Asyrion; Kaelith, the
   Shadow Sentinel, sealed the horde below. Lore strings, banners, the title screen's line and the lore cards may name
   these now. Every other name from the lore waits for the owner (M8), and still no gods are invented.
2. **Six pieces from the lore, each answering to magic** (GDD: Floors and rooms, From the Eda lore): the watching statue,
   the sapping obelisk, the drinking ward, the rope bridge over a chasm, currents with a waterfall to feed them, and candle
   stands that stand in water. Each is a mechanism or a tile in the room kit's style, with the templates that use it.
3. **The Wandering Merchant of Belerial**: a second, rare trader, once a run in an ordinary room after its fight, with a
   pack beast and a crow (wares the Bound Shade doesn't sell; the crow sells the floor's map or the secret room).
4. **Three bosses built in code** (GDD: Bosses from the Eda lore): the War-Tiger (armoured plates that blasts and Shatter
   break off), the Pillar Dragon (coils round the room's pillars, fire on one side and frost on the other) and the Twin
   Guardians (two statues, one shielding while the other strikes). They join the floors' pools where they fit (the
   Tiger and the Guardians the Reliquary's and the vault's, the Dragon the Shore's or a new floor's), a run still never
   meeting one twice.
5. **Fen-Nex**: the Rune Fox familiar made Asyrion's companion by an artifact, growing into its full shape as the run
   goes on.

**In later milestones** (GDD roadmap): Asyrion's arc from apprentice to chaos mage and Boketto's look for the Stormcaller
in M4.6 (costumes and transformations; the hero's Tripo model from the owner's sheets); Dr. Jelly's laboratory, and the
Verdant school with its garden rooms, in M5; the Vigil at the cave mouth over the valleys, its shades (Boketto, the Cloud
Forest Wizard, the Huruhui foragers), Kaelith as the final boss and a won run ending in daylight in M7; the rest of the
lore's names in M8.

## Steps

1. **The setting in the text** (built): the title screen names the place (`title.place`, beneath the Gate of Uzulim) and
   Asyrion; floor 1's banner opens beneath the Wardens' chapel, floors 3 and 4 name the Wardens; the Hollow Warden is "a
   Warden of Uzulim who never came back up"; the vault and the secret cell are theirs; the end screen names Asyrion, and a
   won run Kaelith's seal still holding below; the bone key, the chest, the lodestone shard, the gilded reliquary, the
   Sexton's Keyring and Consecrate name the Wardens in their lore. `STR`, the lore data and one line of CSS; a browser test
   reads the title, the first banner, the Warden's and the end screen.
2. **Watching statues and sapping obelisks** (built): two mechanisms (`w` and `!` in templates; looks in `mechLooks.ts`,
   what they do in `mechanisms.ts`, tuning in `CFG.mech.statue` and `.obelisk`). The statue's head follows the mage; while a
   fight lasts, the mage in its sight (no wall, rock, pillar or ice between) charges it over 2.2 s, then it marks the spot
   and its ray strikes there 0.75 s later (half a heart; foes standing there are struck too). A hex blinds it for 7 s: a
   Hex Bolt striking it, or Grasp of the Grave's hands closing near (`umbralAt`). The obelisk's ward is one of the five
   schools, drawn on the room's seed and shown in its runes and field; inside its field the cantrip strikes half as hard
   (`sapMul` in `spawnBolt`) and mana drains 5 a second, and its school landing on it breaks the ward: a bolt carrying the
   status, fire, frost or shock landing (`elementAt`), holy ground or Judgement's pillar (`hallowAt`), Grasp's hands. Bolts now
   carry every status to what they strike (`strikeSolid`), and the rune stone keeps to the elemental ones. Five templates:
   `gaze_watch`, `gaze_hall`, `obelisk_court`, `obelisk_pair`, `warden_vigil`. The dev menu lists both with the objects and
   adds holy light and darkness to fire, frost and shock ahead (`__game.element('sanct' | 'hex', x, z)`); `__game.props`
   reads `charge`, `blind`, `aiming`, `ward` and `broken`, `__game.sap` the cantrip's worth where the mage stands.
3. **The drinking ward** on a vault door and as a boss's shield. As designed: every vault's ward drinks the spells cast
   near it (within reach of the sealed door: the spell's mana goes into the ward, and the spell itself is lost), counting
   what it drank by school; full, it bursts, the vault opens, and a Wardling of the school it drank most rises inside as
   the vault's guard. A bone key still unbinds the ward, and two glyph blasts still force it and wake the floor's vault
   warden, so there are three ways in and the mage chooses the fight. The Wardlings are five foes in code, one per school
   (a round body of liquid light with big eyes and its school's crest, floating; they keep their distance and fire volleys
   carrying their school: Ember lights what it passes, Frost chills, Storm is fast, Hallow comes as a ring, Umbral hexes),
   resisting their own school. On a foe (`e.ward`), the ward drinks the spells that strike it instead of taking their
   damage, and full, it breaks and a Wardling of the school it drank most joins the fight; the Twin Guardians (step 8)
   are the first boss to wear it, and the dev menu wards any foe to try it before then.
4. **Rope bridges and chasms; currents and waterfalls; candle stands in water** (tiles and pieces, the Landing's river
   given a current, templates).
5. **The Wandering Merchant of Belerial** (the merchant's look and the beast's, the wares, the crow).
6. **The War-Tiger.**
7. **The Pillar Dragon.**
8. **The Twin Guardians.**
9. **Fen-Nex.**
10. **Close:** the GDD as built, CLAUDE.md, the rules reviewer, the room inspector's sweep, and a build for the owner
    when asked.

Every step gets its dev menu entries and browser tests, and is committed and pushed.
