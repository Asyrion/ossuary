# M4.2 Beneath the Gate: plan

The owner's ask (2026-09-30), after reading what their Eda lore drop (PR 24: notes and 27 images) suggests for the game
(`docs/ideas.md`, The Eda lore drop): "I like all your strongest ideas takeaways. Let us do all of them and add them to the
GDD if they fit." They are in the GDD (Vision: Setting; Combat and enemies: Bosses from the Eda lore; Magic system: a
sixth school; Artifacts: Asyrion's arc, Fen-Nex; Potions and alchemy: Dr. Jelly's laboratory; Floors and rooms: From the
Eda lore; Pickups and room types: the Wandering Merchant; RPG progression: the Vigil). This milestone builds the ones that
belong now; the rest go into the milestones they fit (below).

## Decisions (2026-09-30; the owner can change any of it)

1. **The setting comes into the game's text.** The dungeon is the Uzulim Wardens' vault beneath the Gate of Uzulim, in the
   Pass of Buluiain; the chapel above is theirs; the Hollow Warden is a fallen Warden; the mage is Asyrion; Kaelith, the
   Shadow Sentinel, sealed the horde below. Lore strings, banners, the title screen's line and the lore cards may name
   these now. Every other name from the lore waits for the owner (M8), and still no gods are invented.
2. **Six pieces from the lore, each answering to magic** (GDD: Floors and rooms, From the Eda lore): the watching statue,
   the sapping obelisk, the drinking ward, the rope bridge over a chasm, currents with a waterfall to feed them, and candle
   stands that stand in water. Each is a mechanism or a tile in the room kit's style, with the templates that use it.
3. **The Wandering Merchant of Belerial**: a second, rare trader, once a run in an ordinary room after its fight, with a
   pack beast and a crow (wares the Bound Shade doesn't sell; the crow sells the floor's map or the secret room).
4. **Three bosses built in code** (GDD: Bosses from the Eda lore): the War-Tiger (armoured plates that blasts and Shatter
   break off), the Pillar Dragon (coils round the room's pillars, fire on one side and frost on the other) and the Twin
   Guardians (two statues, one shielding while the other strikes). They join the floors' pools where they fit (the
   Tiger and the Guardians the Reliquary's and the vault's, the Dragon the Shore's or a new floor's), a run still never
   meeting one twice.
5. **Fen-Nex**: the Rune Fox familiar made Asyrion's companion by an artifact, growing into its full shape as the run
   goes on.

**In later milestones** (GDD roadmap): Asyrion's arc from apprentice to chaos mage and Boketto's look for the Stormcaller
in M4.6 (costumes and transformations; the hero's Tripo model from the owner's sheets); Dr. Jelly's laboratory, and the
Verdant school with its garden rooms, in M5; the Vigil at the cave mouth over the valleys, its shades (Boketto, the Cloud
Forest Wizard, the Huruhui foragers), Kaelith as the final boss and a won run ending in daylight in M7; the rest of the
lore's names in M8.

## Steps

1. **The setting in the text** (built): the title screen names the place (`title.place`, beneath the Gate of Uzulim) and
   Asyrion; floor 1's banner opens beneath the Wardens' chapel, floors 3 and 4 name the Wardens; the Hollow Warden is "a
   Warden of Uzulim who never came back up"; the vault and the secret cell are theirs; the end screen names Asyrion, and a
   won run Kaelith's seal still holding below; the bone key, the chest, the lodestone shard, the gilded reliquary, the
   Sexton's Keyring and Consecrate name the Wardens in their lore. `STR`, the lore data and one line of CSS; a browser test
   reads the title, the first banner, the Warden's and the end screen.
2. **Watching statues and sapping obelisks** (built): two mechanisms (`w` and `!` in templates; looks in `mechLooks.ts`,
   what they do in `mechanisms.ts`, tuning in `CFG.mech.statue` and `.obelisk`). The statue's head follows the mage; while a
   fight lasts, the mage in its sight (no wall, rock, pillar or ice between) charges it over 2.2 s, then it marks the spot
   and its ray strikes there 0.75 s later (half a heart; foes standing there are struck too). A hex blinds it for 7 s: a
   Hex Bolt striking it, or Grasp of the Grave's hands closing near (`umbralAt`). The obelisk's ward is one of the five
   schools, drawn on the room's seed and shown in its runes and field; inside its field the cantrip strikes half as hard
   (`sapMul` in `spawnBolt`) and mana drains 5 a second, and its school landing on it breaks the ward: a bolt carrying the
   status, fire, frost or shock landing (`elementAt`), holy ground or Judgement's pillar (`hallowAt`), Grasp's hands. Bolts now
   carry every status to what they strike (`strikeSolid`), and the rune stone keeps to the elemental ones. Five templates:
   `gaze_watch`, `gaze_hall`, `obelisk_court`, `obelisk_pair`, `warden_vigil`. The dev menu lists both with the objects and
   adds holy light and darkness to fire, frost and shock ahead (`__game.element('sanct' | 'hex', x, z)`); `__game.props`
   reads `charge`, `blind`, `aiming`, `ward` and `broken`, `__game.sap` the cantrip's worth where the mage stands. From the
   room inspector: the statues are pale limestone (they stood black against the lit north wall), the ray is a straight gold
   beam (`beam`), the gaze line near white (it vanished on the Reliquary's gold floors), and each obelisk floats its
   school's sign over its point (a flame, a snowflake, a bolt, a halo, a crescent: Hallow's gold and Storm's yellow were
   too close). An obelisk's field ring shows beyond low walls, so templates keep obelisks well inside the room.
3. **The drinking ward** on a vault door and as a boss's shield (built). As designed: every vault's ward drinks the spells cast
   near it (within reach of the sealed door: the spell's mana goes into the ward, and the spell itself is lost), counting
   what it drank by school; full, it bursts, the vault opens, and a Wardling of the school it drank most rises inside as
   the vault's guard. A bone key still unbinds the ward, and two glyph blasts still force it and wake the floor's vault
   warden, so there are three ways in and the mage chooses the fight. The Wardlings are five foes in code, one per school
   (a round body of liquid light with big eyes and its school's crest, floating; they keep their distance and fire volleys
   carrying their school: Ember lights what it passes, Frost chills, Storm is fast, Hallow comes as a ring, Umbral hexes),
   resisting their own school. On a foe (`e.ward`), the ward drinks the spells that strike it instead of taking their
   damage, and full, it breaks and a Wardling of the school it drank most joins the fight; the Twin Guardians (step 8)
   are the first boss to wear it, and the dev menu wards any foe to try it before then. Built so: the rules are the pure
   `magic/drinking.ts` (unit-tested); the vault's in `world/vault.ts` (`wardNear`, `drinkSpell` from `castSpell`; a disc of
   light behind the ward's rune fills with the colour of what it drank most; `CFG.ward.need` 60 mana); the foe's in
   `actors/wardShield.ts` (`wardFoe`, `drinkBlow` from `damageEnemy` for blows `by` a spell; `inflict` skips a warded foe;
   `CFG.ward.foeNeed` 60 of harm). The Wardlings are `wardEmber` to `wardUmbral` in `data/foes.json` (floor 0: no floor's
   own; the `wardling` behaviour in `foes.ts`, the look `wardling(school)` in `modelOptions.ts`, names in `STR.options`); the
   dev menu lists them after the floors' foes and has "Ward the nearest foe"; `__game.wards` reads every vault's ward and
   warded foe, `__game.wardFoe(i)` wards one.
4. **Rope bridges and chasms; currents and waterfalls; candle stands in water** (built). The rope bridge is `+` in a
   template (a run of them across or down is one bridge, `bridge` in `propData.ts`, its look in `objectLooks.ts`): it lies
   over the chasm's dark (`addPit` without its box), the mage and walkers cross it, and fire burns it through in 4 s; then
   the chasm opens under it (`openChasm`), a foe on it falls, and the mage takes a heart's fall (`CFG.props.fallHurt`) and
   climbs out on the nearest ledge. A template's `current` (n, s, e, w; mirrored with the grid) makes all its water run
   (`room.flow`): it carries the mage and walking foes, bolts and shots over it and pickups lying in it downstream
   (`CFG.flow`), its surface flows, frozen it stops, and where it leaves the wall upstream a waterfall pours from a spout
   (`buildFalls` in `pools.ts`; a room whose water runs grows no puddles). A candle stand in water is `L` (`wetCandles`, its
   tile part of the pool): lit and counted as any candle stand, it throws a pool of light on the water, and shock running
   through the water puts it out (`douseStandsIn`). Three templates: `rope_chasm`, `mill_race` and `flooded_hall`; the
   Ferry Landing's river runs (`landing_moor` south, `landing_long` east). The dev menu's objects list both new pieces;
   `__game.flow` reads the room's current and its waterfalls.
5. **The Wandering Merchant of Belerial** (built). Once a run, from the second floor on, an ordinary room's cleared fight
   calls the merchant with a chance of 0.14 (`CFG.wanderer`, the run's own `wanderer` stream). The merchant rides in
   through the door furthest from the mage on the pack beast (`wandererLook` in `specialLooks.ts`: an old trader with a
   white beard, a turban and a staff with a bell, on a shaggy beast with a red snout, bandaged legs, packs and a rolled
   blanket, a crow on its head). Once the beast stops, the wares come out in the shade's ward circles (`layWare`,
   `merchant.ts`): a focus the run hasn't met from any pool (`pickFocus`), a black or hallowed soul mote from the packs,
   and the crow's wares (`crowWareLook`): the floor's map (every room on the minimap) and, while there is one to find,
   where the secret room lies (on the map, its cracked walls glowing). Prices are in `data/loot.json` (`wanderer`). The
   merchant is gone when the mage leaves the room (`wandererLeaves` in `enterRoom`). The dev menu's Rooms & runs calls the
   merchant into the room; `__game.callWanderer()` and `__game.wanderer` (here, wares out, which).
6. **The War-Tiger** (built). A white-maned tiger with grey stripes built in code (`tigerLook` in `bossLooks.ts`, Frost),
   armoured in four plates: a bronze pauldron on each shoulder, a slab of carved stone along its back and a bronze brow,
   each with a frost rune. It joins the pools of floors 3 and 4 (`data/floors.json`), calling the floor's walkers below half
   health. It stands as a statue in its hall (eyes shut, a stone tint, no light about it, no contact) until the mage comes
   within 3 tiles or strikes it, so a glyph can be chalked beside it first; then it shakes off the stone and roars. It
   prowls at the mage and, in reach of its claws, circles them rather than walking into them; its cycle is a swipe when the
   mage is close (a forepaw raised, then a fan of claws) or a pounce when not (it crouches, tail lashing, the spot it will
   land on marked on the floor; a wall or rock in the way stuns it; frost shards fly from its plates as it lands), and a
   roar every third (a ring; a second below half health). Its plates let only 40% of a blow through, more with each one
   lost, all of it with none; a plate falls to a glyph's blast, to a Shatter (bosses never freeze, so a shock on its plates
   rimed by two chills shatters one), or once 30 damage has been turned since the last fell; each plate lost makes it 14%
   faster. Weak to Storm (it fears the Wardens' bells), resisting Frost (its plates rime). Tuning in `CFG.enemy.tiger`.
   Its fight is in the dev menu's boss list and its look in the Galleries tab; `?boss=tiger`.
7. **The Pillar Dragon** (built). A long dragon built in code (`pillarDragon.ts`), orange down its left flank and
   blue-grey down its right, cream beneath, with antlers, a spiked mane, a beard and long whiskers; its head is one look
   (`dragonHead`) and its body 22 segments drawn as instances (`dragonBody`: three draw calls), laid each step along the
   trail its path leaves (`dragonPath.ts`, pure). It joins floor 5's pool beside the Angler, calling the floor's flyers
   below half health. It comes down from above onto the pillar nearest where it stands; a room with fewer than three
   pillars raises four out of the floor at its quarter points (`raisePillar`, where clear), and a new boss room, the
   belfry (`boss_belfry`: four pillars, two bells), may be drawn for any boss. Coiled round a pillar's top it leans out
   towards the mage, and only its head can be struck (its body is wound round the stone); it breathes down on the floor
   twice, fire and frost by turns (its maw glows with what is coming, its head drops, then a stream sweeps across where
   the mage stood: burning shots that light oil, chilling shots that freeze water), then rears back and flies to another
   pillar (the one nearest the mage two times in three), its body unwinding after it; below half health it throws a ring
   of fire and frost as it lands. A glyph's blast at the foot of its pillar, or a bell rung in the room, knocks it off: it
   crashes into a heap on the floor and lies there four seconds, open (half again of each blow, and it hurts to touch),
   then climbs the nearest pillar. Weak to Umbral, resisting Ember. Tuning in `CFG.enemy.dragon`. Its fight is in the dev
   menu's boss list and its look (coiled round a column) in the Galleries tab; `?boss=dragon`, `__game.dragon`.
8. **The Twin Guardians** (built). Two colossal bearded guardians of sandstone built in code (`twinLook`: lion paws for
   hands and feet, a helm, a round stone shield on the left arm, eyes of the Wardens' gold; the second greyer, its beard
   forked, a crest on its helm), Hallow; weak to Frost, resisting Hallow. They join the pools of floors 3 and 4 and call the
   floor's heavies below half health (once, for the pair). The first built raises its twin beside it (`twinGuardians.ts`).
   They crouch as statues, their heads turning after the mage (as the Uzulim statues' eyes follow whoever comes near),
   until the mage comes within 3.2 tiles of either or strikes one; both wake together, and the one nearer the mage takes
   up the shield. The shielding twin raises its shield and a drinking ward and holds its post: the cantrip glances off it,
   and the ward drinks spells (full, it breaks into a Wardling and spells reach it). The striking twin stalks the mage,
   stopping at arm's length, and by turns slams the floor (it rears, then a ring of stone and a blow beside it) and fixes
   the mage with its gaze (its eyes blaze, then a stream of gold straight at them). Every six seconds the shield passes
   across. They share one boss bar; when one falls the other rages (no shield, faster, sooner to strike), and the fight's
   reward comes once, when the second falls. Built first with the shielder putting itself between the mage and its twin
   (the GDD's "they trade places as the mage moves"): it body-blocked every line on the striker and the fight ran to twice
   the others', so it holds its post for the owner's play-test. Tuning in `CFG.enemy.twins`; the dev menu's boss list and
   the Galleries tab (the pair); `?boss=twins`.
9. **Fen-Nex** (built). A new artifact, the Rune of Fen-Nex (a disc of indigo stone with a fox's face cut in rune-light;
   treasure, boss, vault and merchant pools), binds the Rune Fox the owner drew as Fen-Nex (`src/combat/fennex.ts`). It
   keeps at the mage's heel, behind and a little to the side of where they aim, and darts at the nearest foe to bite it
   (the cantrip's damage times 0.6, 0.85 and 1.1 by stage), then comes back. It grows a stage for each floor gone down since
   it was bound (`G.run.fennexFrom`): the small rune fox; full-grown, its bite burning (Ember); then its full shape, built
   in code (`fenNex` in `modelOptions.ts`: a long white beast between fox, cat and dragon, its fur blown back into
   flame-tipped feathers, its legs plated dark blue with red spiral runes, a tail burning at its tip), which also breathes a
   fan of Ember at a foe near it. A toast says when it grows. Left behind in another room it comes to the mage's side.
   Tuning in `CFG.artifacts.fennex`. The dev menu's Mage tab gives the artifact (from the data) and grows it a stage; the
   Galleries tab's familiars show both shapes; `__game.fennex`, `__game.growFennex()`. The three beast artifacts that make
   its pelt a transformation wait for M4.6.
10. **Close:** the GDD as built, CLAUDE.md, the rules reviewer, the room inspector's sweep, and a build for the owner
    when asked.

Every step gets its dev menu entries and browser tests, and is committed and pushed.
