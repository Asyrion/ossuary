# M4.9 The curve: plan

Proposed on 2026-10-01 in the review the owner asked for after M4.5 (GDD: Depth for what's built, M4.9). It waits on the
owner's yes, and its place in the order is theirs to move.

Floors 2 to 5 differ from floor 1 mostly in their foes and their foes' health (`enemyHp` ×1.15 to ×1.6 in
`data/floors.json`) and in how often a room is hard. This milestone turns the descent's pressure into dials. It makes
sure every build has a way through each floor, and it measures balance instead of guessing it.

## What the review measured

Each school against each floor's foes, weighted by the floor's rolls:

| Floor | Weak to it | Resist it | Drink it (elementals) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1, the crypt | Ember 46%, Frost 41% | Storm 48% | none |
| 2, the drowned | Storm 52% | Frost 58% | Frost 6%, Storm 5% |
| 3, the burning crypt | Frost 53% | Ember 51% | Ember 23% |
| 4, the Gilded Reliquary | Frost 46% | Storm 27%, Frost 26% | Ember 7%, Storm 7% |
| 5, the Ferryman's Shore | Ember 47%, Hallow 23% | Umbral 37%, Frost 36% | Frost 4% |

Every floor has a school it rewards and a school it walls off. That is good for variety, but a build can stall: an Ember
mage meets floor 3, where three in four foes resist or drink fire.

## Decisions to take with the owner

1. **How much the libraries lean.** In this plan, at least one page on a floor's lectern is of a school that floor's
   foes are weak to, if the mage doesn't already hold one. The other choice is to only weight the draw.
2. **The banner's warning.** Whether a floor that walls off the mage's school says so on arrival, or the player is left to
   find out.

## Steps

1. **A balance bot in the repo** (first, so the later steps are measured).
   - `tools/balance.ts` (`npm run balance`) is the M4.5 bot made a tool. It runs Playwright against a dev server: for
     each foe on its floor, a walking, shooting bot fights it alone, two seeds each, once with the cantrip only and once
     with each school's spells.
   - It writes time to kill and hearts lost to `docs/balance.md` and flags any foe past twice its floor's median, or any
     school that more than doubles a floor's median.
   - Options: `-- floor=3`, `-- foe=hydra`, `-- school=frost`.
   - Not in CI (it takes minutes). The rules reviewer asks for it when `data/foes.json` or a behaviour changes.
2. **Pressure dials.**
   - `pressure` per floor in `data/floors.json`: foes added to a room's waves (a share of rooms gets one more of the
     template's roles), the elite and champion chances, an ordeal's waves, and the chance of a pair (M4.7).
   - Read in `buildRoom` and `assignTemplates`. The generator's unit tests hold the dials rising from floor to floor.
   - The dev menu: Rooms & runs shows the floor's dials.
   - The bot of step 1 sets the targets: a floor's average room should take about a fifth longer than the floor above
     it, for the same build.
3. **The floor teaches its counter.**
   - The floor's weak schools are worked out from its rolls (a pure `floorWeakness(table)` in `foeData.ts`,
     unit-tested).
   - `pickPages` and the merchant's stock take them as a lean (decision 1).
   - When the mage's slotted spells are all of a school the floor's foes mostly resist or drink, the floor's banner adds
     a line (`STR.floors` lines per school, such as "The heat here feeds on fire").
   - A browser test: floor 3 with only Ember spells offers a Frost or Storm page in its library.
4. **Hex reads.**
   - A hexing blow that lands flashes the mana bar violet (`renderSpells` in `hud.ts`), and the mana it took runs from
     the bar back to the foe that threw it in a violet trail.
   - The first time in a run, a line says what happened (`STR.lore` or a message, as the full-health pickups do).
   - A hexed mage's next Hallow cast gives back what the last blow took.
   - The dev menu: Magic gets "Hex me", which lands a hexing shot on the mage.
5. **The sluice.**
   - A mechanism for water rooms (`MECHANISMS` in `propData.ts`, letter `Z`). A bolt or a blast opens it. The room's
     pools drain to mud for `CFG.mech.sluice` seconds: wading still slows, but the lurker, serpent and newt can't hide.
     Then it closes and the pools fill again.
   - Its look in `mechLooks.ts`: an iron gate in a stone frame, with water falling through it while it is open.
   - The drowned floor's water templates gain one sluice each where they have room for it.
   - The dev menu: Foes & objects puts one down.
   - A browser test: an open sluice brings a Cistern Lurker up.
6. **Close.** Run the bot on all five floors and tune its outliers. The GDD as built (and `docs/balance.md`), CLAUDE.md,
   the rules reviewer, and the room inspector on the sluice.

**Done when** `npm run balance` measures every foe and school; the dials rise from floor to floor and the bot shows each
floor a little harder than the last; a mage with one school is offered a counter on the floor that walls it off; a hexing
blow reads; and every water room can be drained by any build.
