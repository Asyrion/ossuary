# M4.7 Living foes: plan

Proposed on 2026-10-01 in the review the owner asked for after M4.5 (GDD: Depth for what's built, M4.7). It waits on the
owner's yes, and its place in the order is theirs to move. The bestiary gave every foe a counter. This milestone makes
those counters something the mage learns. It also makes foes part of the room, not only in it. It adds no new creatures:
the depth comes from what is built.

## Decisions to take with the owner

1. **The five mantles and their names** (placeholders until M8): Kindled, Rimebound, Stormcharged, Hallowed and
   Hexbound. One mantle a champion, drawn from the floor's schools. The opposite school strips it.
2. **Whether foes step round hazards.** In this plan, beasts blunder into other foes' ground, while golems and
   elementals step round it. The other choice is that every foe blunders.
3. **The rising words for counters.** Each counter gets one word ("Grounded!", "Snuffed!", "Flipped!" and so on), in
   `STR.foes.counters`.

## Steps

1. **Champion mantles.**
   - Data: `mantles` in `data/magic.json` (the school, what it gives, the school that strips it, how long the stun lasts).
     A floor's `champion` weights in `data/floors.json` say which mantles it draws.
   - `src/actors/mantles.ts` (pure rules, unit-tested) and the champion's look: its aura in the mantle's school light,
     replacing the ember tint, with a rune ring under it.
   - The rules. Kindled leaves burning ground (`layFire`) and is immune to Burn. Rimebound's contact and shots chill,
     and it is immune to Chill. Stormcharged sparks at the mage when struck (`laySpark` under the mage). Hallowed mends
     the foes round it, a little a second, but not while it is Sanctified. Hexbound's shots drain mana (`hex`), and it
     hexes the room as it falls.
   - Stripping. The opposite school's status strips the mantle: the word "Unbound!" rises (`STR.foes.unbound`), the
     champion is stunned for the set time and takes ×1.25 while stunned.
   - The dev menu: Foes & objects gets "Champion with mantle", one button per mantle, ahead of the mage.
   - A browser test per mantle: what it gives, and that its opposite strips it.
2. **Foes in each other's ground.**
   - `bestiaryGround.ts`: `stickyAt` and `slipAt` take a walker, not only the mage. Webs and ink slow foes, rime makes
     them slide (a foe's own step through the same grip rule the mage uses), and burning ground (`burningNear`), sparks
     (`laySpark`) and spores sting them.
   - Ground carries its maker's `owner` and kind, so a foe's own ground spares it and its kind.
   - `avoidsGround` on a foe def (golems, elementals): their steering picks the side step away from a hazard.
   - The dev menu: "Lure test" in Foes & objects, which lays a strip of each ground between the mage and a wolf pack.
   - A browser test: wolves cross a Magma Ooze's trail and burn; a Salt Golem steps round it.
3. **Counters found.**
   - Each behaviour's counter gets an id and a word (`STR.foes.counters`), and a `counter(e, id)` call where the counter
     happens. Examples: the griffin's `afflicted` hex, the djinn's snuffing, the crab's `blasted`, the phoenix's ashes put
     out, the hydra's seared stump, the basilisk blinded.
   - `bestiary.ts` keeps `counters: string[]` per foe beside `weak` and `resist`. The first time a counter happens, its
     word rises (`floatText`) and the record keeps it.
   - The inventory gains a Foes tab (`TABS` in `inventory.ts`). It lists the foes met (name, school, weakness and
     resistance if found), the counters found as lines, and sealed lines for the counters not yet found. Text comes from
     `STR.bestiary`, plus a counter line per foe (`STR.foes.counterLines`).
   - The dev menu: the knowledge row also finds every counter or forgets them all (`__game.findCounters()`).
   - Tests: a unit test of the record, and a browser test that a hex on a griffin opens its line and the Foes tab shows
     it.
4. **Rooms with a pair.**
   - `pairs` in `data/floors.json`: a list of `[foeA, foeB, weight]` per floor, a chance a room rolls a pair, and which
     roles they fill.
   - `buildRoom` places the pair's two foes in two of the template's roles, side by side (`freeSpotNear`). The pure pick
     goes in `foeData.ts`, unit-tested on hundreds of seeds, and keeps the rule of one elite and one pack a room.
   - The first pairs: a Salt Golem with a Basilisk, a Choir Lark with Rime Wolves, a Moonstag Fawn with a Gargoyle
     Whelp, a Cinder Djinn with Gloom Ravens, a Glass Newt with a Thunderhead (sparks over its water), and a Crypt Spider
     with an Ink Octopus (webs and ink).
   - The dev menu: Rooms & runs lists "Pairs", which builds a test room of each.
5. **Close.** Sounds for the mantles and the words. The GDD as built, CLAUDE.md, the rules reviewer, the room
   inspector on the champions' looks, and the bot (M4.9's `npm run balance`, if built first) on the mantled champions.

**Done when** a champion's mantle reads at a glance and its opposite strips it; foes cross or avoid each other's ground
as their kind does; a counter found once is known in the Foes tab in the next run; and a floor rolls a few pairs.
