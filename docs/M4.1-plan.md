# M4.1 Two floors deeper: plan

The owner's ask (2026-09-30, after M4): "create 2 more floor concepts and add them to the game, with new torches,
braziers, enemies and other assets; with two new floors we also need more artifacts, spells and items for our pool."
It comes straight after M4, before M4.5 (Bestiary) and M4.6 (Costumes). Every name is a placeholder until the Eda lore
arrives; the lore stays in the dungeon (chapel, crypts, sextons, pilgrims, the Ferryman) and invents no gods, factions
or places.

## Decisions (2026-09-30; the design is this milestone's, the owner can change any of it)

1. **A run is five floors.** The two new floors come after the Burning Scriptorium, deeper under the chapel: floor 4,
   the Gilded Reliquary, and floor 5, the Ferryman's Shore. Beating floor 5's boss wins the run. Room counts rise a
   little (15 to 19 on both), foes' health goes on rising (×1.45, ×1.6), and deeper floors roll more hard rooms.
   Floor 4 has a shrine as well as floor 2 (curses weigh more deeper down). Undoing this is data: the floors list.
2. **Two floors, two looks, each the opposite of the other** (and of the three above, warm stone, damp green and
   scorched brick):

   | Floor | Name (banner) | Where | Light | Walls | Floors | Wall lights | Braziers |
   | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
   | 4 | The Gilded Reliquary: where the chapel hid its relics | Pale limestone and veined marble, gold leaf, old bronze, candle wax everywhere | White-gold (Hallow) | marble, gilded panels, blind tracery | marble chequer, gold inlay, mosaic | gilded sconce, candle crown, candelabra | chalice, standing censer, font |
   | 5 | The Ferryman's Shore: where the dead wait to cross | Black slate, wet river stone, tarred pilings and boards, salt crust, cold mist | Pale teal and violet spirit fire (Umbral) | slate, drystone, pilings | landing boards, black shingle, flags | boatman's pole lamp, cairn light, witchlight | stone boat, funeral urn, basket |

   Each new kind of wall, floor, wall light and brazier is built in code in the room kit's style (`pixelTextures.ts`,
   `fireLooks.ts`), drawn per room like the others. The Reliquary's floor has wax drips (`decor: wax`) and gilt on its
   walls (`stains: gilt`); the Shore's floor has puddles and its walls salt (`stains: salt`).
3. **Eight foes, four a floor, each a new kind of fight** (from the idea bank's Bestiary where one fits; round, big-eyed,
   a school's light, smaller than the mage but the elite):

   | Floor | Foe | School | Role | Fights by | Weak | Resists |
   | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
   | 4 | Wickmouse | Ember | R | a mouse with a candle stub on its back: darts from cover to cover, then at the mage; lights oil it runs through, and its candle falls burning when it dies | Frost | Ember |
   | 4 | Chime Bat | Storm | W | flits in loops; its chime marks the ground under the mage, and a spark strikes the mark a moment later | Frost | Storm |
   | 4 | Gargoyle Whelp | Frost | H | perches as stone (bolts glance off it); drops when the mage comes near and pounces twice, landing with a ring of frost, then turns to stone again | Storm | Frost |
   | 4 | Moonstag Fawn | Hallow | W | keeps away from the mage; bows its glowing antlers and heals the foes round it; put it to rest first | Umbral | Hallow |
   | 5 | Rootling | Umbral | R | burrows, a mound creeping after the mage, and bursts up under it; fire on it or its mound keeps it above ground | Ember | Umbral |
   | 5 | Storm Ray | Storm | W | a manta gliding over the room in long passes, leaving a line of sparks on the floor that shocks the water it crosses | Frost | Storm |
   | 5 | Void Mote | Umbral | W | a dark orb with eyes that drifts and swallows the mage's bolts, growing with each, and spits them back as a ring; a spell bursts it | Hallow | Umbral |
   | 5 | Lantern Tortoise (elite) | Frost | H (one a room) | a big tortoise carrying a little shrine of candles; hides in its shell (bolts glance off) and spins across the room off the walls, then rests out of it, open to harm | Storm | Frost |

   Hallow and Umbral damage count once the spells below exist: a Moonstag Fawn or a Void Mote is then best met with the
   school it fears.
4. **Two bosses built in code**, one a floor, as the Warden is (a floor never repeats a boss the run has met, so five
   floors need five bosses):
   - **The Hallowed Hart** (floor 4, Hallow): a great white stag with candles burning in its antlers. It walks, lowers its
     antlers and charges along a line lit on the floor (stunned against a wall), turns in place firing spokes of light,
     stamps a ring of motes; below half health it calls Moonstag Fawns, which heal it while they live.
   - **The Deep Angler** (floor 5, Umbral): a great round anglerfish floating in the mist, its lure a lamp on a stalk. The
     lure brightens and draws the mage in towards its jaws (run against it) and it bites; it spits shadow orbs that hex;
     it drifts under the dark while the room's lights gutter round its lure; below half health it calls Void Motes.
     Striking it while it draws the mage in breaks the pull.
   - Bosses take affinities from here on: the Hart is weak to Umbral and resists Hallow, the Angler the other way round.
5. **Hallow and Umbral become schools the mage can cast** (GDD: Magic system), with a status each and the two reactions
   the GDD plans:
   - *Sanctified* (Hallow): for 4 s the foe takes a quarter more damage from everything, and nothing heals it.
   - *Hex* (Umbral): stacks up to 5, fading 6 s after the last; each hit the mage lands on a hexed foe gives back mana
     (3 a stack), and a foe that falls with three or more stacks leaves a soul mote (the new items below).
   - *Eclipse* (Hallow + Umbral): the foe is taken out of the fight for 2 s under a black sun, then struck hard.
   - *Blackfire* (Umbral + Ember): a violet burn that never goes out until the foe dies.
   - Six spells, three a school, four from the GDD's table and two new ones:

   | School | Spell | Shape | Does |
   | --- | --- | --- | --- |
   | Hallow | Consecrate | zone | holy ground at the mage's feet for 6 s: foes in it are sanctified and burn with light; the mage in it heals half a heart once |
   | Hallow | Judgement | smite | after a second's warning, a pillar of light on the strongest foe in the room: great damage, sanctified |
   | Hallow | Wardlight (new) | orbit | six motes of light circle the mage for 8 s, breaking enemy shots and sanctifying foes they touch |
   | Umbral | Hex Bolt | bolt | a slow dark bolt that hexes (two stacks) |
   | Umbral | Grasp of the Grave | grasp | hands rise from the floor where the mage aims and hold every foe there for 2 s, hexing them |
   | Umbral | Ferryman's Toll (new) | mark | the nearest foe is marked with a coin of shadow: every hit on it returns mana, and if it falls within 6 s it pays an obol and a hex spreads to its neighbours |

   Libraries and bosses offer them like the others; each gets its effect in the house style (`docs/effects.md`: the
   gallery's Hallow pillar and Umbral hex are their starting point), its sounds in its school's palette, and its lore.
6. **Twelve new artifacts**, eight from the idea bank and four for the new schools and floors:

   | Artifact | Kind | Does |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | Pendulum of Hours | Focus | bolts swing out in a curve, left on one cast and right on the next |
   | Lodestar Wand | Focus | bolts stop where their range ends and hang for 2 s as small stars that hurt foes who touch them |
   | Twin Vigil Candles | Passive | standing still, bolts strike half again as hard; two candle flames brighten at the mage's shoulders |
   | Ossuary Choir | Passive | every fifth foe put to rest in a room makes the choir sing: every foe in the room takes 5 damage |
   | Echo Stone | Passive | a bolt that strikes a wall echoes back the way it came, once |
   | Blood Moon Pendant | Passive | at half health or less, bolts fly faster and come quicker, in crimson |
   | Salt Circle | Passive | standing still for a second rings the mage in salt that walking foes can't cross |
   | Gilded Halo (new) | Passive | the first bolt to strike each foe sanctifies it |
   | Ferryman's Lantern (new) | Passive | hexed foes that fall give back half again the mana, and soul motes come twice as often |
   | Hart's Antler (new) | Passive | every sixth bolt is a lance of light that pierces and sanctifies |
   | Gravemonger's Contract | Cursed | gift: every foe put to rest drops an obol; price: room clears drop no hearts, and hearts cost twice as much |
   | The Last Rite | Cursed | gift: once a floor, a killing blow is refused and the mage rises with half a heart; price: one heart container less |

   Each has a look in `artifactLooks.ts` (its HUD icon rendered from it), lore, and the pools it appears in.
7. **Items: soul motes** (the idea bank's Isaac soul hearts), a new pickup family on every floor, most on the new ones:
   pale flames beside the hearts in the HUD that take a hit before the hearts do and can't be healed back. A *pale mote*
   guards one hit; a *black mote* guards one hit and hexes every foe in the room when it is lost; a *hallowed mote* is
   always lost last and gives back half a heart at the floor's end if it survives. When mana runs short, a spell can burn
   a soul mote for the rest (a use for them beyond guarding). They drop from rooms and remains, the merchant sells them,
   and hexed foes leave them. Also a **lodestone shard** (Isaac's battery): it finishes every spell's cooldown and gives
   25 mana. More families (scrolls, talismans) stay in the idea bank for later.
8. **Two themed rooms for the new floors** (the twelve go on being drawn where they fit): the *Gilded Treasury* (floor 4:
   heaps of old offerings that spill obols to a blast, reliquary chests, candle stands; its twist: taking its gold wakes
   its guardians) and the *Ferry Landing* (floor 5: the river runs through the room between boards and moored boats, lamps
   on poles; its twist: the drowned rise from the water while the fight lasts, until the river is frozen).

**Magic take:** the Reliquary answers to light (candles everywhere to light, a Hallow boss and Umbral that hurts it), the
Shore to the dark and water (spirit fire, the river that carries shock and freezes, an Umbral boss and Hallow that hurts
it); the new schools reach into the rooms as the others do (Consecrate lights candle stands, Blackfire burns through
oil).

## Steps

1. **Five floors** (built 2026-09-30). Floors 4 and 5 in `data/floors.json` (rooms 15 to 19 each, health ×1.45 and ×1.6,
   more hard variants, templates, themes drawn from the twelve, a shrine on floor 4) with their looks' colours and light,
   first with the older floors' kinds, and the rotling standing in for their foes until steps 5 and 6; banners; the run
   ends after floor 5 (it went by `FLOORS.length` already); the lists of floors in the dev menu and the foe validator come
   from the data. Floor 5 holds a little fewer vaults and more artifacts in its secret room than floor 4, so its obols
   stay in the GDD's 12 to 18 a floor (it has no shrine to take a fight's place). Browser test: down from floor 3 to a
   win on floor 5.
2. **The two bosses** (built 2026-09-30, moved up: five floors need five bosses, as a run never meets one twice). The
   Hallowed Hart and the Deep Angler in `bossLooks.ts` (`hartLook`, `anglerLook`) and `bosses.ts` (`updateHart`,
   `updateAngler`), tuning and affinities in `CFG.enemy` (`bossAff` on the boss; `damageEnemy` reads it; the bestiary
   keeps bosses beside foes), the mage's `pull` (the lure's draw, spent each step), their sounds and names; both in the
   Galleries tab beside the Warden's looks. Browser tests: each beaten, its affinities, the lure drawing the mage in.
3. **The Reliquary's kit** (built 2026-09-30): walls of veined marble, gilded panels and blind tracery; floors of marble
   chequer and brass inlay (and the mosaic); a gilded sconce and a crown of candles; a gilt chalice and a standing censer;
   gilt flecks on its walls (`stains: gilt`) and spilt wax on its floors (`decor: wax`). The kit's browser tests build
   every kind on every floor. The room inspector over it with the Shore's (step 4).
4. **The Shore's kit** (built 2026-09-30): walls of split slate, dry river stones and tarred pilings; floors of landing
   boards and black shingle (and flags); a boatman's lamp on a crooked pole and a cairn with a flame in its top stone; a
   stone boat and a funeral urn; a faint salt crust on its walls (`stains: salt`) and puddles on its floors. The room
   inspector over both new floors.
5. **Floor 4's foes** (built 2026-09-30): the Wickmouse (`scamper`), Chime Bat (`chimer`), Gargoyle Whelp (`gargoyle`) and
   Moonstag Fawn (`healer`), from look to foe, with sounds; a behaviour may leave something behind as its foe falls (`die`,
   called from `killEnemy` through `foeDied`: the wickmouse's candle). Floor 4's roles: wickmice walk, chime bats (three in
   four) and fawns keep off, gargoyles are its heavies and the vault's warden; the Hart's summons are the floor's flyers, so
   its fawns heal it. Browser test: each foe's fight.
6. **Floor 5's foes** (built 2026-09-30): the Rootling (`burrower`: under the floor as a mound, bursting up under the mage;
   burning, it stays up), Storm Ray (`glider`: long passes laying sparks that arm, shock water and sting; the foes' floor
   hazards are one list of two kinds now, fire and sparks, each drawn as one instanced mesh), Void Mote (`absorber`:
   swallows the cantrip, a behaviour's `struck` hook in `damageEnemy`; full, it spills a ring and hangs open) and the
   Lantern Tortoise (`shell`, the elite: hides and spins off the walls with frost at each bounce, then lies dizzy). Floor
   5's roles: rootlings walk and dig, storm rays (two in three) and void motes keep off, the tortoise and the rootling are
   its heavies; the tortoise guards its vault. Browser test: each foe's fight.
7. **Hallow and Umbral** (built 2026-09-30): three statuses more in the pure rules (`magic/status.ts`, unit-tested):
   Sanctified (a quarter more damage taken, no healing: the fawn's light passes it by), Hex (stacks to 5; each blow the
   mage lands spends one for 3 mana) and Root (held in place, still striking; a boss only for a moment), and the two
   reactions: Eclipse (the foe is out of the fight for 2 s under a black sun, nothing reaches it, then it is struck for
   18; a boss is only struck) and Blackfire (a violet burn that never goes out). Six shapes more in `CAST`: `zone`
   (Consecrate: holy ground that strikes and sanctifies what walks in it and heals the mage once), `smite` (Judgement: a
   closing ring of gold on the strongest foe in reach, then a pillar of light), `orbit` (Wardlight: six motes that go
   with the mage, break the shots they meet and sear what they touch), `bolt` (Hex Bolt, through the spell bolts),
   `grasp` (Grasp of the Grave: hands that rise where the mage aims and close) and `mark` (the Ferryman's Toll: a coin of
   shadow over the nearest foe; each blow on it gives 4 mana, and falling marked it pays an obol and spreads its hex).
   Their looks are in `spellFx.ts` in the house style, their sounds in each school's palette; the bosses' and foes'
   Hallow and Umbral affinities now meet the mage's own spells. The dev menu's spells list them by themselves, and its
   statuses row lays Sanctified, Hex and Root on every foe (Eclipse and Blackfire by hand). Browser test: each spell
   cast, both reactions, a hex's mana and the toll's obol.
8. **Soul motes and the lodestone shard** (built 2026-09-30): four items more (`paleSoul`, `blackSoul`, `hallowedSoul`,
   `shard` in `items/loot.ts`), with the pure rule for which mote goes first (`soulToLose`: the last taken that isn't
   hallowed) and a floor's `souls` weighting in `data/floors.json` (×2.5 on floor 4, ×3 on floor 5: about 0.4 motes a floor
   above, 1.1 to 1.3 below, besides the merchant's). `items/souls.ts` carries them out: `player.souls` beside the hearts in
   the HUD (flames in each mote's colour), `guardHit` in `hurtPlayer` before the hearts, a black mote's hex on every foe in
   the room, `burnSoul` when a spell is cast short of mana (said once a run), `hallowedMend` at every floor's end. A foe
   that falls with three hex stacks or more leaves a black mote one time in two (`CFG.souls`). The shard ends every
   cooldown and gives 25 mana, and is left lying while there is nothing to give. Taking an item and the merchant's
   refusals now share one rule (`cantUse`, `useItem` in `pickups.ts`). Looks in `pickupLooks.ts` (a flame with its heart,
   a halo on the hallowed one, a violet ring round the black; a splinter of lodestone veined in blue), lore, sounds, the
   inventory's items tab, and the dev menu's resources row (one of each mote, none, a floor's end). Holy light now kindles
   candle stands too (`hallowAt` in `mechanisms.ts`, from Consecrate and Judgement). Browser test: each mote's rule, the
   spell that burns one, the shard, and a hexed foe's mote. Found on the way: a fawn the hart called healed it a quarter of its health
   at a time, faster than the mage could hurt it; a boss now takes only a little of that light (`bossShare`, 4%).
9. **The twelve artifacts** (built 2026-09-30): data in `data/artifacts.json` (57 now, the wand among them), a look each in
   `artifactLooks.ts` (the HUD icon rendered from it) and a drawn icon for the old look, tuning in `CFG.artifacts`. What
   they reach into: the Pendulum's `swing` path (a bolt sets out to one side and turns evenly back onto the aim line at its
   range's end, the side alternating by cast) and the Lodestar's stars (`star` on a bolt: it stops at its range and hangs),
   the Echo Stone (`echoed`: once back off a wall), the Gilded Halo (`haloed` on a foe) and the Hart's Antler (every sixth
   cast pierces and sanctifies) in `projectiles.ts`; the Twin Vigil Candles and the Salt Circle from `player.still` (seconds
   standing still), their flames and ring in `familiars.ts` (`saltHolds` from the foes' movement); the Blood Moon at half
   health (`bloodMoon()`: faster bolts, a shorter cast delay, crimson); the Ossuary Choir (`room.choir`, a hymn a beat after
   the fifth foe), the Ferryman's Lantern (hex mana, `soulsHere`), the Gravemonger's Contract (its obols in `killEnemy`, its
   price in `onRoomCleared` and the merchant's `priceNow`) and the Last Rite (`G.run.riteFloor`, in `hurtPlayer`). The
   Gravemonger's gift is one foe in two, not every one: with every foe paying, a floor would give twice its obols.
   Browser test: each artifact's rule.
10. **The two themed rooms** (built 2026-09-30): the *Gilded Treasury* (`treasury`, floor 4: gilded walls, an inlaid
    floor, gold light, spilled obols scattered, candle stands round a sealed reliquary) and the *Ferry Landing* (`landing`,
    floor 5: piling walls, boards, lamps on poles, a river through the room with boats moored at its banks), two templates
    each (`treasury_vault`, `treasury_great`, `landing_moor`, `landing_long`). Two objects: the offering heap (`h`, low;
    bolts clink off it, a glyph's blast spills it into obols: `hoard` in `data/loot.json`, one item a heap, so a floor
    stays within the GDD's 12 to 18 obols) and the moored boat (`m`, low, a run of letters one boat, and it burns), both
    in the room kit's look only. Two twists in `themes.ts`: `guardians` (the treasury's foes stand as statues, `statue` on
    a foe, that closeness doesn't wake; taking an obol there wakes them all at once, `goldTaken` from taking a coin; a hit
    wakes one) and `drowned` (while the fight lasts, the floor's flyers and shooters climb out of the river, at most three,
    until every pool of it is frozen; `CFG.themes.drown*`). Two scatter kinds, spilled `coins` and `lamps`. Floors 4 and 5
    draw on them (`themes` in `data/floors.json`). The dev menu's test rooms list them by themselves; "Objects here" lays
    its fifteen objects out eight to a row. Browser test: the statues, the heap's blast and the waking, the drowned and
    the frozen river. The owner's lore drop (the flooded hall with candles standing in the water) fits the Landing: for
    later, candle stands in its water.
11. **Close:** GDD (as built), CLAUDE.md, the rules reviewer, a closing sweep by the room inspector over both floors,
    and a build for the owner when asked.

Every step gets its dev menu entries (floors in the new-run list, kit kinds in the Look tab, foes by floor, bosses,
spells, artifacts and drops all come from the data; the rest by hand) and browser tests, and is committed and pushed.

**Checks:** each new floor reads at a glance and differs from the three above; a fight on floor 5 within its draw calls
and at 60 fps in the dev menu's readout; every foe and boss telegraphs, dies without console errors and meets the rooms'
reactions; the run can be won on floor 5.
