1. ***Lore:***
	- People that try to live in harmony with nature
	- Use [[Magic|magic]] for working with nature, not manipulating it for their benfit
	- Neutral, peace-loving people
	- Democratic nation states
		- Leaders are elected of all working people in the tribe / city / dwelling
	- Their [[Magic|magic]] mostly deals with the conservation and understanding of water and wildlife
		- Water because they use hydroponics
		- Nature / Wildlife because they utilize animals as a workforce
		- Healing because nature provides the best blueprints for healing [[Magic|magic]]
	- [[Azyrion - The Protagonist|Player]] is raised in a [[The Huruhui - Forest Dwelling People|Huruhui]] village
	- For most people / folks of [[Eda - The Planet Earth|Planet Eda]] they seem primitive because they use less technology
	- Different pagan [[The Source - Deity|religion]] than most other cultures, no direct deities except a mysterious [[The Source - Deity|Source]] as they call it
	- Unconcerned about everything outside of their [[Huruhui - Homeland Concept|valleys, forests and plains]]
	
2. ***Appearance:***
	- Headgear:
		- Most citizens just wear flower wreaths made out of different kinds of colorful flowers
		- Kind of flowers indicate social standing => different kinds of flowers are only to be used by special professions
		- Just warriors and leaders wear some kind of wooden / bone helmet
	- Body:
		- Robes and cloths made of silk and linen
		- Clothes are adorned with petals and flowers, same flowers as in the flower wreaths
		- Insignias on their clothes corresponding to their profession and learned trades
		- Lots of belts and buckles for storage
	- Weapons & Tools:
		- Mostly different kinds of spears as they are used as tools and weapons and for walking stabilization
			- Spears get customized throughout their life and imbued with magic sigils 
		- A kind of magic boomerang for hunting
		- Different magical tools for getting ores and foresting

3. ***Sounds:***
	- Normal human voices, english
	- Flowery enchanted background music
	- Sound of trees whistling in the wind

4. ***Abilities:***
	- No special abilities - they never appear as enemies
