7. Variations:
	- _**Zenko**_, or good foxes, are benevolent **kitsune** who protect and help humans. They are usually white or golden in color, and can have up to nine tails.
	- _**Yako**_, or field foxes, are the most common type of **kitsune**. They are not particularly good or bad, and can be mischievous or malevolent, depending on the situation. They can have up to nine tails.
	- _**Tsukimono-suki**_, or shape-shifters, are **kitsune** who can transform into any form they desire. They are known for using this ability to seduce men or trick humans.
	- _**Hyakki-yakō**_, or night parade foxes, are a type of **kitsune** that are associated with the _**Hyakki Yakō**_, an army of spirits and demons that is said to march at night. These kitsune are known to be particularly malevolent and dangerous.
