[A Living Seed-Creature is a type of organism that has a fully synthetic and radically altered DNA code**1**](https://www.theguardian.com/science/2019/may/15/cambridge-scientists-create-worlds-first-living-organism-with-fully-redesigned-dna). It can grow from a seed into a complex life form with various abilities and adaptations. [One example of a Living Seed-Creature is an acorn seed that can bloom into a humanoid creature with plant-like features**2**](https://www.artstation.com/artwork/KOZZmX). Here is a possible description and image of such a creature:

The creature has a slender body covered with green scales that resemble leaves. Its head is shaped like an acorn, with two large eyes and a small mouth. It has long arms and legs that end in sharp claws. It also has a long tail that can whip around like a vine. The creature can use photosynthesis to produce energy from sunlight, and can also communicate with other plants through chemical signals. It is curious and playful, but also wary of predators and threats.
