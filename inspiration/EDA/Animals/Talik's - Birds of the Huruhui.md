1. ***Lore:***
	- Some species are used by the [[The Huruhui - Forest Dwelling People|Huruhui]] as messenger birds similiar to pigeons
		- Messenger birds get decorated via chains, pearls and other jewelry to mark their status and owner
	- "*Ta*" in Huruhui means "*Wing*" 
	- "*Lik*" means "*to sing*"
		- Talik then means something like "*Wing to sing*" or "*Singing Wing*"

	- Some look like fruits with wings
	- Appearances oriented towards paradise / tropical birds of earth
