A Book of Mispells is a rare and dangerous tome that contains spells that have gone wrong in some way. Some of the mispells are harmless, such as turning a frog into a log, but others are deadly, such as summoning a demon that cannot be controlled. The book itself is old and worn, with pages torn and stained with blood and ink. The cover is made of leather and metal, with a large lock that requires a special key to open. The book radiates a dark and chaotic energy that attracts the curious and the foolish.
