___

- Player starts with an empty world map
- Player needs to find a “magic feather” to note down his path on his world map
- Cartographers are found throughout the world wo sell parts of maps they explored
    - There are different kind of completion ratios for cartographers maps
    - Some are just outlines and some are as detailed as the whole map + locations of certain items / ressources
- Cartographers maps can sometimes also be found somewhere or maps of lost treasures can be found
